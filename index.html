Our story

Built for students building a life in Australia.

GracePath Education Consulting was founded in May 2026 to make the move from application to enrolment feel manageable for students choosing Australia as their next classroom, wherever in the country that journey leads, and for families backing that decision from overseas.

We work closely with universities, colleges and English-language providers across Australia, and we sit alongside a network of partner MARA-registered migration agents and immigration lawyers, who handle formal visa assessment, documentation and lodgement. That means every student gets education guidance and a properly qualified migration pathway, without having to coordinate the two themselves.

Our approach is personal by design: one consultant, one student, one plan — from the first conversation about courses through to your first week of class.

What we do

Guidance for every stage of your move to study in Australia.

From choosing a course to settling into your first semester, we work one step at a time so nothing about the process feels like guesswork.

Course & university selection

We match you to the right course and institution based on your goals, academic background and budget — not a one-size-fits-all shortlist.

Application & enrolment support

We prepare and lodge your institution applications, track offer conditions, and manage the paperwork so deadlines never catch you out.

Visa pathway guidance

We prepare you for what's ahead and coordinate directly with our partner MARA-registered migration agents and immigration lawyers, who handle your formal visa assessment and lodgement.

Scholarships & funding advice

We help you identify relevant scholarships, understand fee structures, and prepare the financial evidence your application and visa will need.

Pre-departure & settlement support

Orientation, accommodation pointers and local know-how, so your first weeks in Australia are about settling in, not scrambling around.
